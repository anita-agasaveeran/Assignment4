| 10 | **Guard rails** | The stop rule hides the band per note source or country; shortlist futility pause; gates on all wines; a row without a note source never counts toward the 3-source minimum; names (country, grape, winery, …) matched to the training spelling before scoring; simulated on development folds | Simulated pilots on masked lockbox notes: shortlist deployed 97.5%, band shown 16%. False pauses at most 3% in development pools; a random ranker is paused 97–100% of the time at 200 wines. The band is hidden for 44–94% of wines in a unit that is really off, 0–5% of others. Misses units under 50 wines; one- or two-writer pilots stop without a decision (§6.1) |
| 11 | **Monitoring** | Input-drift checks each round, including countries and wineries unseen in training and a file pre-filtered at $25; two-sided ranker-level alerts whose lower limits come from a stated rule on out-of-fold development rounds, per round size (0.08 and 3% for an A7 round of about 1,000 ≤ $25 wines); a self-referenced rule (share above P90 below half its median over 3 accepted rounds, from 600 ≤ $25 wines; an alerted round stays out of the reference, so a persistent fault keeps alerting); false alerts 0% on A7-sized development and lockbox rounds, at most 2.4% on fresh out-of-fold rounds; outcome checks as scores arrive; a quarterly business KPI; no automatic retraining; integrity check on the model file, through the serving path | Deliberately broken feeds, A7 rounds: caught in 99–100% of rounds: truncation to 30, 25 or 15 words (the milder two by the self-referenced rule only), 5%+ unknown words, prices × 1.3, lost grape list, unrecognised countries, boilerplate, a pre-filtered file. Missed: milder truncation (35 or 40 words: the share falls by a third or less), 2% unknown words (1.5%), prices × 1.1 (0%), and truncation before round 4. The outcome checks stay essential (§6.2, `deployment/monitoring_plan.md`) |
