## Score: 7 / 10 · Major issues remaining: yes

The analytical core of Phase 6 is strong, and it is mostly honest. The artifact really does reproduce the lockbox. The pilot protocol is code, not prose: `winevalue.pilot` serves both the simulations and `pilot_eval.py`. Its parameters are set on development pools, and the simulation reports unflattering results next to flattering ones: the band is shown in 14.5% of masked-lockbox pilots, Gregutt is over-withdrawn in 99%, and France in fold 3 is never checked. The departures from §5.3 are listed in a table, with a reason for each. Several of those departures are real fixes: the LB pause trigger would have stopped working shortlists in 9–33% of pilots, and the pilot wines have to be chosen without the model.

The weaknesses are where a deployment review should be hardest: the gap between what the plan says the tool does and what the tool does.
- `band_status.json` reports a withdrawn pooled unit, but neither the app nor `score.py` reads that flag.
- The integrity check can be bypassed, and a silent rebuild overwrites the manifest.
- None of the per-round monitoring and data-quality checks in §6.2 exists in code.
- The shortlist tier labels are wrong by about 3× on held-out writers.
- The plan never says where the outcome (a later critic score for a retailer candidate) comes from, how candidates are matched to reviews, or how long it takes to reach 400 scored wines. So the deployment plan has no schedule.

## Most important improvement
Make the deployed tooling do exactly what the report and the simulations say it does, and prove it with one integration test.
- **Stop rule (issue 2):** one shared `band_hidden(df, status)` function that covers the pooled unit and normalises names.
- **Integrity (issue 3):** a loader that refuses a missing manifest and never rebuilds without the reproduction check.
- **Monitoring (issue 1):** a per-round monitoring and data-quality report with its thresholds in `config`.
- **Tier labels (issue 4):** labels that are true on held-out writers.

The test should run `evaluate_raw` on the dry-run pilot file, feed the status it writes into `score_round` and the app's band logic, and assert that the wines hidden are exactly the ones `run_protocol` hides. Then commit and tag that state. Phase 6's statistics are good enough. What a professional reviewer would not accept is a pilot whose guard rails exist in the simulator but not in the tool the buyers will use. Next in priority is the outcome-feed and timeline plan (issue 5), because without it the pilot can't start.
