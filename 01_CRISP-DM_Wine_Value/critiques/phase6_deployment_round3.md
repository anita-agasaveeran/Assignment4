## Most important improvement
Fix issue 1: canonicalise the inputs, not just the group labels, and refuse to count a missing source.

- **Countries:** map `country` to the model's training categories (name key plus aliases) before scoring. Flag and alert on any country still unseen.
- **Sources:** leave "unknown" out of the ≥ 3-source minimum.
- **Tests:** prove both on the real artifact.
  - A round with lower-case or alias countries must give identical scores, gate and withdrawals.
  - Two writers plus unsourced rows must stay "interim".

Revision 3's claim is that "a typing variant can't split a unit or meet the source minimum". That now holds for note sources. But a typing variant in the country column still changes the predictions, the gate and the stop rule without any alert, and a blank source column still meets the minimum.

After that, decide the price basis (issue 2) as a rollout prerequisite and re-validate the lower alert limits out of fold (issue 3). Then the phase is ready to commit and tag.
